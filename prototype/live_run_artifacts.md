# 1. STRUCTURED BRIEF

## Problem Statement

Small, independent property management companies (managing 100–500 units) are hemorrhaging tenants and leasing prospects because of slow, manual response to maintenance requests and leasing inquiries. This is not a motivation problem—it is a capacity problem. A two-person operation managing 200 units cannot respond to every 11pm "my heat is out" message in a timely way.

**The math is brutal:**
- Tenant turnover costs average **$4,000 per unit per turnover event** ([https://allbetterapp.com/8-biggest-pain-points-for-property-managers/](https://allbetterapp.com/8-biggest-pain-points-for-property-managers/))
- Turnover costs have **more than doubled since COVID**, often exceeding one full month's rent before marketing and vacancy losses are counted ([https://www.joinbeagle.com/post/10-property-manager-pain-points](https://www.joinbeagle.com/post/10-property-manager-pain-points))
- **39% of property managers** spend more than 20 hours per month just on maintenance requests ([https://lula.life/articles/ai-property-management-software](https://lula.life/articles/ai-property-management-software))
- Maintenance issues are the **most common tenant complaint**, with response time as the critical satisfaction driver ([https://www.secondnature.com/blog/tenant-complaints](https://www.secondnature.com/blog/tenant-complaints))
- Operational costs consume **35–45% of gross rental income** ([https://allbetterapp.com/8-biggest-pain-points-for-property-managers/](https://allbetterapp.com/8-biggest-pain-points-for-property-managers/))

## Target Customer

**Primary:** Independent property management companies in the U.S. managing 100–500 units, typically with 2–6 staff members, no in-house tech team, and no dedicated leasing coordinator.

**Market size:** Approximately **335,293 property management businesses** exist in the U.S. as of 2025 ([https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/number-of-businesses/property-management/1356/](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/number-of-businesses/property-management/1356/)). **35% manage 101–500 units** ([https://www.apmrealestate.com/post/the-differences-between-small-and-large-property-management-companies](https://www.apmrealestate.com/post/the-differences-between-small-and-large-property-management-companies)), yielding an addressable pool of roughly **117,000 companies**.

**Psychographic:** Owner-operators who got into property management for real estate, not software. They are overwhelmed, skeptical of SaaS bloat, and will pay for something that demonstrably saves them money or headaches—but not for a dashboard they have to babysit.

## Core Insight

Enterprise platforms like EliseAI claim **90% automation of leasing conversations** (unverified vendor claim) ([https://eliseai.com/blog/ai-property-management-software-costs](https://eliseai.com/blog/ai-property-management-software-costs))—but they are priced and structured for large operators. The small PM company has **no path to that technology** without a six-month implementation and an enterprise contract. You are the path.

You are not building software. You are building and operating a **done-for-you AI response system**, configured for each client, monitored by you, and sold as a managed service. The client gets the outcome (fast responses, documented requests, fewer missed leads) without touching a single API.

# 2. OFFER DEFINITION

## Product Name: **TenantReply** *(working title—rename to taste)*

## What It Is

A **fully managed AI communications layer** that sits between tenants/prospects and the property management company. It handles:

1. **Maintenance request intake** — Tenants text, email, or submit via a web form. The AI responds immediately, collects structured information (unit number, issue type, urgency, photos), logs the ticket, and sends a confirmation with an expected response window. Urgent issues (no heat, flooding, gas leak) are immediately escalated via SMS to the PM's phone.

2. **Leasing inquiry response** — Prospects who inquire via email, website form, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, or Apartments.com receive an immediate, personalized AI reply: availability, pricing, pet policy, next steps, and a link to schedule a showing. No lead sits cold overnight.

3. **Automated follow-up sequences** — Prospects who don't book a showing get a follow-up at 24 hours and 72 hours. Maintenance tickets with no resolution update get a "we haven't forgotten you" message at 48 hours.

4. **Weekly digest report** — Every Monday, the PM receives a plain-English summary: tickets opened, tickets resolved, leads received, leads converted, average response time, and any flagged issues requiring human attention.

## What It Is NOT

- It is not a full property management platform (no accounting, no lease signing, no payment processing)
- It is not self-serve software the client configures
- It does not replace the PM's judgment for complex decisions, legal matters, or negotiations

## Core Value Proposition

> *"Every tenant message answered in under 5 minutes, 24/7. Every leasing lead followed up automatically. You find out when something actually needs you."*

## Quantified ROI for the Client

# 4. GO-TO-MARKET

## Phase 1: Land the First 3 Clients (Days 1–30)

The goal of Phase 1 is **proof of concept and testimonials**, not revenue optimization. Offer the first 3 clients a discounted or free pilot in exchange for a detailed case study and a 10-minute video testimonial.

### Channel 1: Direct Outreach — LinkedIn + Email

**Target identification:**
- Search LinkedIn for "property manager" + city, filter by company size 1–10 employees
- Cross-reference with Google Maps searches for "property management [city]" — look for companies with 3–4 star reviews mentioning slow responses (this is your smoking gun)
- Use Apollo.io or Hunter.io to find direct email addresses

**Outreach message (LinkedIn DM — 75 words max):**

> *"Hi [Name] — I help small property management companies automate their maintenance request intake and leasing inquiry follow-up using AI. Tenants get a response in under 5 minutes, 24/7. Managers stop playing phone tag. I'm looking for 2–3 companies in [city] to pilot this for free in exchange for feedback. You'd need to spend zero time on setup — I handle everything. Worth a 15-minute call to see if it fits?"*

**Daily outreach target:** 20 LinkedIn DMs + 10 cold emails. Do this every weekday for 30 days.

### Channel 2: Google Reviews Mining

Search Google Maps for property management companies in your metro. Filter for those with reviews that say things like:
- "Never responds to maintenance requests"
- "Took weeks to fix..."
- "Hard to reach"
- "Ignored my calls"

These are **pre-qualified buyers**. They have public evidence of the exact problem you solve. Reference it tactfully in outreach:

> *"I noticed some of your tenants have mentioned response times in reviews — this is incredibly common for companies managing 150+ units without a dedicated coordinator. I've built something that solves exactly this..."*

### Channel 3: Local NARPM Chapter Outreach

The National Association of Residential Property Managers (NARPM) has local chapters in most metro areas. Attend one meeting as a guest (typically free), lead with curiosity not pitch, and offer to present a free "AI for small PM companies" educational session at the next meeting. This positions you as an expert and puts you in front of 20–50 warm prospects at once.

### Channel 4: Facebook Groups

Property management Facebook groups (search "property management [state]," "landlord [city]") are active and full of small operators complaining about exactly the problems you solve. Do not pitch. Instead, answer questions helpfully for 2 weeks, then post a value piece: *"I set up an AI system that responds to maintenance requests in under 2 minutes — here's exactly how I built it (and what I'd do differently)."* Include a Calendly link at the bottom for those who want help implementing it.
