The idea: Build and sell a done-for-you AI intake system that captures, qualifies, and follows up with every after-hours lead so small law firms never lose a prospect to slow response.

The offer

A fully installed, practice-area-tuned AI intake agent (voice + web form) that answers after-hours calls and form submissions, qualifies the lead with jurisdiction- and case-type-specific questions, and fires a warm summary plus auto-follow-up to the attorney within minutes — delivered in 5 business days, managed ongoing for a flat monthly retainer. Includes a live dashboard showing every lead captured, response time, and estimated case value. Priced as setup fee plus monthly, no revenue share.

How you'd sell it

Cold-DM or cold-email 50 personal injury and family law solo/small firms in one metro using their Google Business missed-review complaints and slow response signals as the opener, offering a free 14-day pilot with zero setup fee in exchange for a testimonial.
