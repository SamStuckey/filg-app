### The interview script (ask about the past, never pitch the idea)
1. Tell me about the last time you tried to figure out what to sell / nail down your offer. What did you actually do?
2. What did that cost you — time, money, tools, courses, an agency?
3. What was the hardest part — the idea, the offer itself, the pricing, or finding buyers?
4. What have you tried to fix it, and what did you pay for?
5. What happened — did it work? What did you do next?
6. Where do you go for this kind of help today?
